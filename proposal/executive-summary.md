We're proposing to finish it. And we're proposing a fixed-fee, not-to-exceed structure: the price you're quoted is the price you pay, delivered in phases so your team sees working software early, not just at the finish line.

We're right-sized for this engagement — no overselling, no orphaned handoff docs. Just a dashboard that works, is tested, is documented, and is finally yours to run with.
