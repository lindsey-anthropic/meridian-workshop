# Executive Summary

**RFP #MC-2026-0417 — Meridian Components, Inc.**

Meridian's inventory dashboard is a strong foundation with room to grow. We understand the priorities: resolve the outstanding Reports module issues, add a Restocking recommendations capability for your operations team, establish automated test coverage so your IT team can approve changes with confidence, and deliver clear architecture documentation for internal use.

Our approach addresses these as a connected set of work rather than a checklist. We build automated test coverage alongside the Reports fixes, so every change is verified as it ships and your team gains lasting confidence in the system going forward. We'll ground the Restocking recommendations in direct conversations with your operations team, so the tool reflects how your business actually makes restocking decisions. Architecture documentation is produced as part of our onboarding review, giving your IT team a clear, current picture of the system.

We propose a time-and-materials engagement with a not-to-exceed cap, giving you budget certainty while allowing us to move quickly. Our technical approach, timeline, and pricing assumptions follow in the sections below.
