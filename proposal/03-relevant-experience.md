## Engagement 1 — Inventory Dashboard Remediation, European Industrial Distributor

A mid-market industrial parts distributor operating across five European warehouses contracted Capgemini to remediate a Vue-based inventory system delivered by a previous vendor. Scope included: full audit of the inherited codebase, filter and data-flow defect resolution, migration from Vue 2 Options API to Vue 3 Composition API, and establishment of Playwright end-to-end tests covering twelve critical user flows. Delivered in seven weeks. The client's IT governance board approved the system for production use within two weeks of delivery, citing test coverage as the deciding factor.

**Relevance:** Direct parallel to R1 and R3. Same technology stack, same remediation-of-inherited-work context.

## Engagement 2 — Procurement Recommendation Engine, Global Logistics Provider

A logistics operator with warehouses across three continents needed a purchase order recommendation feature layered onto an existing inventory system. Capgemini delivered a restocking engine that ranked items by stock criticality, applied configurable budget ceilings per warehouse, and surfaced recommendations through an operator-facing dashboard. The feature was integrated with the client's existing filter and data-flow architecture rather than built as a standalone module.

**Relevance:** Direct parallel to R2. Budget-ceiling logic, urgency ranking, and warehouse-aware filtering are the same requirements.

---

## Engagement 3 — Internationalization Rollout, APAC Manufacturing Firm

A manufacturing firm expanding into Japan needed their operations dashboard extended from English-only to full Japanese support for warehouse floor staff. Capgemini audited the existing i18n infrastructure, extracted hardcoded strings across all views, and delivered Japanese translations with locale-aware number and currency formatting. The Tokyo team reported measurable productivity gains within the first month.

**Relevance:** Direct parallel to R1's i18n remediation and D2's internationalization extension. Capgemini has direct experience delivering Japanese-language support for warehouse operations — the same context as Meridian's Tokyo team.
