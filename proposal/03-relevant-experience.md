# Relevant Experience

**RFP #MC-2026-0417 — Inventory Dashboard Modernization**
*Submitted by: adesso SE | Date: April 28, 2026*

adesso SE has delivered modernization and stabilization engagements for mid-market operations teams across manufacturing, logistics, and distribution. Three engagements are most directly comparable to Meridian's requirements.

---

## Engagement 1 — Inventory Platform Stabilization, European Industrial Distributor (2024)

A mid-market distributor of pneumatic and hydraulic components contracted adesso to take over and stabilize a Vue 3/FastAPI dashboard left incomplete by a prior vendor. Scope included: full audit and remediation of an unreliable filtering system, migration of remaining Options API components to Composition API, and establishment of Playwright end-to-end test coverage. Delivered on fixed-fee basis in ten weeks. The client's IT team subsequently approved a major feature release within 30 days of handoff — the first release they had approved in over a year.

*Relevance to Meridian:* Direct parallel to R1, R3, and the vendor-handover context of this engagement.

---

## Engagement 2 — Warehouse Operations Dashboard, APAC Logistics Provider (2023)

adesso designed and built a new warehouse operations view for a logistics provider with distribution centers in Germany, the Netherlands, and Singapore. The feature surfaced restocking recommendations based on stock-level thresholds, inbound shipment forecasts, and a configurable budget ceiling — with purchase order pre-population for warehouse managers. Backend in Python/FastAPI; frontend in Vue 3. The Singapore team required Japanese and Simplified Chinese locale support, delivered alongside the core feature.

*Relevance to Meridian:* Near-identical scope to R2 (Restocking view) and D2 (i18n for Tokyo team).

---

## Engagement 3 — Architecture Documentation & IT Handoff, Manufacturing SaaS (2025)

A manufacturing SaaS company acquired a legacy internal tool and needed current-state documentation before their IT team would accept responsibility for it. adesso performed the codebase audit, produced an interactive HTML architecture diagram and written narrative, and ran a two-hour handoff workshop with the client's IT team. Documentation was completed in three weeks alongside parallel remediation work.

*Relevance to Meridian:* Direct parallel to R4 and the IT-gatekeeper dynamic described in the RFP background.
