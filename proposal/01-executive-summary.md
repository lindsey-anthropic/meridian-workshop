# Executive Summary

**Submitted by:** Capgemini
**In response to:** RFP #MC-2026-0417 — Inventory Dashboard Modernization
**Date:** May 8, 2026

---

Meridian Components has built a capable inventory operation across three warehouses — but the tools your team depends on daily have not kept pace. The dashboard delivered by the previous vendor left the Reports module incomplete, introduced no automated test coverage, and stopped short of features your operations team has been waiting for. The result: your IT team cannot safely approve changes, and your warehouse staff are working around gaps rather than through them.

Capgemini proposes to close that gap in a focused, eight-week engagement. We will audit and resolve all known defects in the Reports module, deliver the Restocking recommendations view your operations team has requested, establish end-to-end test coverage that gives IT the confidence to approve future changes, and produce architecture documentation suitable for long-term handoff.

We have reviewed the source code and previous vendor's handoff notes as part of our response. The existing Vue 3 and FastAPI stack is sound; the problems are specific and well-scoped. We are not proposing a rewrite — we are proposing to finish the job, correctly, with the documentation and test coverage that should have been there from the start.

Our approach prioritizes in that order: unblocking IT (tests first, woven through every deliverable), fixing what is broken (Reports), adding what is missing (Restocking), and leaving Meridian with a codebase their team can confidently maintain.
