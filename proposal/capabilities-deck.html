R1 · Reports Remediation

Fix root causes, not symptoms

We audit the Reports page against the live codebase, group defects by root cause — filter wiring, i18n coverage, data-handling patterns — and resolve each one verified against the test suite we're building in parallel.

R2 · Restocking Recommendations

Built with your operations team, not around them

The Restocking view recommends purchase orders from three inputs: current stock, demand forecast, and an operator-supplied budget ceiling. Before we build the logic, we sit down with the people who'll use it daily to understand how restocking decisions are actually made.

R3 · Automated Browser Testing

Coverage where it matters most

Initial coverage focuses on the Reports module, the new Restocking view, and the shared filter system underneath both — giving your IT team a verifiable foundation on the areas of highest change and historical risk.

R4 · Architecture Documentation

A clear picture for your team

A full architecture review during onboarding produces a current-state overview suitable for IT's ongoing reference — useful today, and for any future vendor transition.
