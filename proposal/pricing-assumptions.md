# Pricing Assumptions

**RE: RFP MC-2026-0417 — Inventory Dashboard Modernization**

The RFP requests pricing (§4.5) but does not publish a budget range. In the absence of that figure, we propose a **phased fixed-fee** structure rather than either a single all-in quote or open-ended time & materials:

- Each phase in our timeline is priced independently, based on the scope confirmed at that point — starting with Phase 1 (Discovery).
- Phase 1's findings inform the fixed fee we quote for Phases 2–4; we are not pricing R1–R4 as one number today, before independently verifying the system.
- Phase 5 (D1–D3) is quoted separately and only after required scope is delivered, so Meridian isn't committing budget to desired items before seeing required ones land.

This gives Meridian per-phase price certainty — which our background notes suggest matters to procurement — without asking them to accept a number built on the previous vendor's incomplete documentation.

**What would change this structure:** if Meridian's evaluation criteria favor a single not-to-exceed figure across the full required scope over phase-by-phase pricing, we can requote Phases 1–4 as one capped numbers once Phase 1 is complete — this is a commercial preference we'd want confirmed with procurement rather than assumed.

**Note:** This document sets out the pricing *structure* and its rationale only. Specific rates, fee figures, and contractual terms are commercial decisions outside this workshop's scope and should be finalized with your engagement's commercial/finance and legal teams before the proposal is submitted to Meridian.
