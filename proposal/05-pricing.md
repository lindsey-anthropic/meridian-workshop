# Pricing

**RFP MC-2026-0417 — Inventory Dashboard Modernization**

Our pricing is structured for **predictability on what Meridian needs** and **control over what is optional**: a fixed fee for the required scope (R1–R4), and time-and-materials with a not-to-exceed ceiling for any desired items (D1–D3) Meridian elects to commission. Figures below are firm for required scope and will be confirmed against the architecture review (R4) before any desired work begins.

---

## 1. Required scope — fixed fee

**Total fixed fee for R1–R4: $96,000.**

A single, predictable number covering the full required scope and the 8-week schedule in our Timeline. Invoiced against acceptance milestones, so payment tracks delivered value rather than elapsed time.

| Milestone | Deliverable | % | Amount |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| M1 | Architecture overview accepted (R4) | 15% | $14,400 |
| M2 | Test suite accepted by IT (R3) | 25% | $24,000 |
| M3 | Reports remediation accepted (R1) | 25% | $24,000 |
| M4 | Restocking accepted + close-out (R2) | 35% | $33,600 |
| | **Total** | **100%** | **$96,000** |

This fee is **fixed**: the risks we identified in our risk register are ours to manage within it, not change orders waiting to happen. That is the predictability Procurement is buying.

---

## 2. Desired scope — time & materials with not-to-exceed

Desired items are optional and billed only if commissioned. Each carries a **not-to-exceed ceiling**, so even on a T&M basis Meridian's exposure is capped before work starts.

- **Blended rate:** $175 / hour.
- **Billing:** monthly, against actuals, never exceeding the per-item ceiling.

| Item | Indicative effort | Not-to-exceed |
|---|---|---:|
| **D1 — UI modernization** | 1.5–2.5 weeks | $14,000 |
| **D2 — Internationalization** | 1–2 weeks | $11,000 |
| **D3 — Dark mode** | 0.5–1 week | $5,500 |
| | **Combined ceiling** | **$30,500** |

Meridian can commission any, all, or none of these — independently — without affecting the required-scope fee or schedule.

---

## 3. Included at no cost — design system & tooling

We bring our own **design system and supporting tooling at no additional charge**: a library of production-ready, accessible, reusable components and patterns we maintain across engagements. This is not a line item — it is value folded into the prices above, and it works in Meridian's favor in three ways:

1. **It lowers the desired-scope cost.** The D1 not-to-exceed is materially smaller than a from-scratch UI refresh would be, because we assemble from proven components rather than building each one. The saving is passed through, not retained.
2. **It de-risks "current standards."** The design system already meets a modern accessibility and consistency baseline, so D1 starts from a mature foundation rather than an undefined target.
3. **It strengthens the required work too.** The same tooling keeps the codebase consistent during R1 remediation and underpins the R3 test suite — improving quality at no extra cost.

---

## 4. What's included vs. excluded

**Included in the fixed fee:** all R1–R4 deliverables; architecture documentation; the full test suite and IT handoff; knowledge transfer at close-out; management of all identified risks.

**Excluded (available separately):** desired items D1–D3 (T&M as above); any net-new scope beyond §3 of the RFP; infrastructure or hosting changes; migration from the JSON-file data layer to a database (we will flag this as a future recommendation in R4 if warranted, with a separate estimate on request).

A known number for what Meridian needs, a hard ceiling on what's optional, and reusable assets that make the whole engagement faster and cheaper than building from scratch.
