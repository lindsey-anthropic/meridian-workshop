# Timeline

**RFP #MC-2026-0417 — Meridian Components, Inc.**

A phased delivery plan, structured to get value to your team quickly. All phases are time-and-materials against a not-to-exceed cap.

## Phase 1 — Onboarding & Discovery (Week 1)

Architecture review of the current system, audit of logged Reports defects, and a discovery session with your operations team on current restocking practice.

## Phase 2 — Reports Remediation + Test Foundation (Weeks 2–3)

Resolve cataloged Reports defects while standing up automated test coverage for the Reports module and shared filter system, so fixes ship verified.

## Phase 3 — Restocking Build (Weeks 3–5, overlapping Phase 2)

Build the Restocking view based on validated recommendation logic from Phase 1 discovery. Test coverage extends to this view as it's built.

## Phase 4 — Documentation & Handoff (Weeks 5–6)

Finalize architecture documentation for your IT team, complete a final regression pass across covered flows, and hand off for review.

## Stretch (if capacity allows)

Internationalization (D2) and dark mode (D3), considered after R1–R4 are complete and verified.
