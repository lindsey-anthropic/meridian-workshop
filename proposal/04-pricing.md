# Pricing

**In response to:** RFP #MC-2026-0417, §4.5 (Pricing)

---

## Pricing model

We propose **time & materials with a not-to-exceed (NTE) ceiling per phase.** This structure is chosen deliberately for this engagement:

- **You get predictability.** Each phase carries a firm NTE ceiling. You will never be invoiced above it, regardless of what the work turns up.
- **You pay for actual effort.** Within the ceiling we bill against real time worked, so if a phase comes in under estimate, you pay the lower actual — not the cap.
- **You carry no overrun risk on the open-ended item.** The Reports defect set (R1) is the one genuinely uncertain scope in this engagement. The NTE ceiling means any defects beyond the logged eight-plus are absorbed by us, not passed back as change orders.

This directly answers the two things the RFP and your buying committee care about most: a complete fix, at a cost you can count on.

## What's included / excluded

**Included:** all delivery effort, regression tests for every fix, the architecture overview, the Restocking feature, project management, and one round of demo-driven revision per phase.

**Excluded (priced separately if requested):** the desired-scope items (Phase 4); standing up a CI pipeline if Meridian has none (we can assist — small, scoped separately); ongoing support/maintenance after final acceptance, available under a separate retainer.

## Payment

Billed monthly against time worked, capped at each phase ceiling. Phase 4 and any support retainer are contracted separately.
