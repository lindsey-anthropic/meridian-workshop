# Pricing

**RFP #MC-2026-0417 — Inventory Dashboard Modernization**

---

The RFP does not specify a budget range, and we've asked Meridian directly for one (see `clarifying-questions.md`). In the meantime, we're proposing a pricing structure rather than a single number, so Meridian can choose the model that best fits their internal budgeting process once that conversation happens.

## Recommended structure: phased fixed-fee

We recommend pricing each phase from the timeline as its own fixed-fee milestone, rather than one lump sum for the full engagement:

| Phase | Scope | Pricing basis |
|---|---|---|
| 0 — Foundation | Data layer migration, architecture doc (draft) | Fixed fee |
| 1 — Stabilize & unblock | Reports remediation (R1), browser testing on Reports (R3) | Fixed fee |
| 2 — Build | Restocking (R2), browser testing on Restocking (R3), architecture doc (final) | Fixed fee |
| 3 — Polish | D1–D3 (UI refresh, i18n expansion, dark mode) | Fixed fee, scoped to remaining budget |

This gives Meridian a firm number to approve before each phase begins, and the option to stop after any required phase (0–2) without losing planned value — Phase 3 is explicitly the first thing to trim if budget is constrained.

## Alternative: time & materials with not-to-exceed

If Meridian prefers flexibility to adjust scope mid-engagement (for example, if the Restocking recommendation logic turns out to need more iteration with R. Tanaka's team), we can instead bill time & materials against a not-to-exceed ceiling set per phase. This carries more schedule risk for Meridian but more flexibility if requirements shift once we're in the data.

## Why we're not proposing a number yet

Pricing responsibly requires knowing Meridian's target range — without it we'd either pad our estimate to cover risk or underbid and renegotiate later, neither of which serves Meridian's stated preference for price predictability. We'd rather firm up real numbers against a known range than publish a placeholder figure now.

*Final pricing, rate cards, and payment milestones will be confirmed once Meridian shares budget guidance, per our clarifying questions.*
