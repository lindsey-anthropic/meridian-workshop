# Pricing

Per the clarifying-questions round, this proposal assumes Meridian prefers price predictability over flexibility — so we're proposing **fixed-fee, not-to-exceed pricing** for the required scope (R1–R4), rather than time-and-materials.

## Structure

- **Fixed fee covers R1–R4** (Reports remediation, Restocking, automated testing, architecture documentation) across the ~9-week timeline.
- **Payment tied to phase completion** (per the timeline: Onboarding, Test Foundation, Reports, Restocking, Documentation) rather than a single lump sum — Meridian sees working software before paying for the next phase.
- **D1–D3 (stretch items)** are scoped as optional add-on effort at an hourly not-to-exceed rate, pursued only if time/budget remains after required scope — not baked into the fixed fee, so they don't inflate the core price or put required-item delivery at risk.

## Assumption

No budget range was published in the RFP. We've assumed a cost-conscious, mid-market budget consistent with Meridian's scale (~$9.6M revenue, 180 employees) and scoped the fixed fee accordingly — favoring a tight, predictable number over padding for unstated flexibility. If Meridian's actual budget expectations differ materially, we'd revisit phase scope (most likely the stretch items) rather than the required-item price.
