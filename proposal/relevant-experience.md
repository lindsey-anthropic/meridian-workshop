# Relevant Experience

**RFP #MC-2026-0417 — Inventory Dashboard Modernization**

---

Our team has repeatedly worked with mid-market operational businesses picking up systems left mid-build by a previous vendor — the exact situation Meridian is in. A few representative engagements (details anonymized per client confidentiality):

- **Regional industrial distributor, multi-warehouse operations (comparable scale to Meridian).** Inherited a Vue/Python inventory system from a prior vendor with an incomplete reporting module and no test coverage. We audited and resolved the outstanding defects, introduced automated browser testing, and delivered architecture documentation to the client's internal IT team — closely mirroring R1, R3, and R4 in this RFP.
- **Distribution/logistics client, demand-driven purchasing.** Built a recommendation feature that translated stock levels and demand forecasts into suggested purchase orders within an operator-defined budget — directly comparable to the Restocking capability (R2) requested here.
- **Multi-region operations team, non-English-speaking satellite office.** Extended partial internationalization support across a dashboard's remaining modules to bring a non-U.S. office fully into a shared tool — comparable to the i18n gap facing Meridian's Tokyo team (D2).
- **Legacy data layer migrations.** Multiple engagements migrating flat-file or ad hoc data storage to a proper database without disrupting the applications built on top of it — the same foundational step we're proposing for Meridian's current JSON-file storage.

Across these engagements, our approach has been consistent: stabilize what exists before extending it, treat test coverage as a prerequisite for velocity rather than an afterthought, and leave the client's internal team with documentation and a codebase they can maintain without us.
